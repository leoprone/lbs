BioClé




Today I will talk about BioClé, a startup from the city of Rosario, Santa Fe, Argentina, founded in 2025.

CONTEXT

There is scientific evidence showing how the composition and diversity of the gut microbiota are closely linked to healthy aging and improved metabolic performance, highlighting the importance of targeted interventions to promote a balanced microbiota and gut health as factors for longevity and quality of life. In this context, butyrate plays a key role in intestinal epithelial function and immune regulation, and its supplementation can sometimes be highly beneficial.

However, much of the active compounds such as butyrate are lost before reaching their site of action. Traditional delivery systems for bioactive molecules, like capsules, release the compounds into the extracellular environment of the target area, dispersing them. This non-specific release can cause adverse effects in off-target tissues and also increases the likelihood of premature degradation or elimination of the active compound, often requiring high doses to achieve the desired effect.

CHALLENGE

In the field of biotechnology and pharmacology, there is growing interest in more sophisticated delivery systems that allow maximizing the effect of bioactive compounds while minimizing doses.









BioClé

SOLUTION

BioClé has developed a delivery platform that protects and transports bioactive molecules directly into the interior of the correct cells, increasing efficacy and reducing the necessary dose in formulations.

HOW IT WORKS

For butyrate as a bioactive compound, BioClé uses GRAS ("Generally Recognized As Safe") bacterial strains optimized to release extracellular vesicles with high yield. Then, those vesicles are loaded with the bioactive compound butyrate, stabilizing it.

Unlike traditional delivery systems that release their content in an extracellular and non-targeted manner, BioClé's vesicles have the capacity to be recognized by colonocytes (target cells) and then endocytosed (internalized) by them, releasing the butyrate only inside those cells. In this way, the bioactive would be protected within the vesicles and released inside the correct cells, reducing premature degradation and absorption, in addition to requiring a lower dose by avoiding losses due to degradation or extracellular dispersion.

OUTLOOK

BioClé is building the technological infrastructure that enables supplement companies, functional foods, and health firms to develop truly effective products, overcoming the limitations of current formats through a high-precision biological delivery system.
